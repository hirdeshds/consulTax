# Vetted Tax Schemes Q&A Corpus

This document provides official vetted information on tax saving schemes, deductions, and exemptions under the Indian Income Tax Act.

---

## Standard Deduction

### What is the Standard Deduction on salary?
Standard deduction is a flat deduction allowed from the salary income of a taxpayer. It does not require any proof of actual expenditure or investments.

### What are the limits for Standard Deduction?
- **FY 2024-2025 (New Regime)**: The standard deduction limit is ₹75,000.
- **FY 2024-2025 (Old Regime)**: The standard deduction limit is ₹50,000.
- Standard deduction is also available to pensioners and family pensioners up to the same limits or the pension amount, whichever is lower.

## Section 80C

### What is Section 80C?
Section 80C is the most popular tax-saving option in India. It allows taxpayers to reduce their taxable income by investing in specific instruments.

### What is the maximum limit for Section 80C?
The maximum deduction limit allowed under Section 80C is ₹1,50,000 (Rupees One Lakh Fifty Thousand) per financial year.

### What are the eligible investments under Section 80C?
Key eligible investments and payments include:
1. Employee Provident Fund (EPF)
2. Public Provident Fund (PPF)
3. Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS) mutual funds
4. National Savings Certificate (NSC)
5. Life Insurance Premium payments
6. Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)
7. Tuition fees for up to 2 children
8. Principal repayment of home loans
9. Tax-saving 5-year fixed deposits (FD)

### Is Section 80C available in the New Tax Regime?
No. Section 80C deductions are only available if you opt for the Old Tax Regime. They are not allowed under the New Tax Regime.

## Section 80D

### What is Section 80D?
Section 80D provides a tax deduction for health insurance premiums paid, preventive health check-up costs, and medical expenditures.

### What are the limits under Section 80D?
- **Self, Spouse, and Dependent Children**: Up to ₹25,000 per financial year (₹50,000 if self or spouse is a senior citizen, age 60+).
- **Parents**: Additional deduction up to ₹25,000 (₹50,000 if parents are senior citizens).
- **Preventive Health Check-up**: Up to ₹5,000 within the overall limits mentioned above.
- **Medical Expenditure**: Senior citizens not covered by health insurance can claim deduction up to ₹50,000 for actual medical treatment expenses.
- **Maximum Possible Benefit**: A taxpayer can claim up to ₹1,00,000 in total under Section 80D (e.g., ₹50,000 for self-family as senior citizens and ₹50,000 for senior parents).

### Is Section 80D available in the New Tax Regime?
No. Section 80D deductions are only available under the Old Tax Regime.

## Section 80CCD(1B)

### What is Section 80CCD(1B)?
Section 80CCD(1B) allows an additional tax deduction for self-contribution towards the National Pension System (NPS) Tier-1 account.

### What is the limit under Section 80CCD(1B)?
Taxpayers can claim a deduction of up to ₹50,000 under Section 80CCD(1B). This is over and above the ₹1,50,000 limit available under Section 80C.

### Is Section 80CCD(1B) available in the New Tax Regime?
No. Section 80CCD(1B) is not available under the New Tax Regime.

---

## Section 80CCD(2)

### What is Section 80CCD(2)?
Section 80CCD(2) allows a tax deduction for the employer's contribution to a taxpayer's National Pension System (NPS) account.

### What are the limits under Section 80CCD(2)?
- **Private Sector Employees**: Up to 10% of basic salary + dearness allowance (DA) under the Old Regime, and up to 14% of basic salary + DA under the New Regime.
- **Government Employees (Central & State)**: Up to 14% of basic salary + DA under both Old and New Tax Regimes.

### Is Section 80CCD(2) available in the New Tax Regime?
Yes! Section 80CCD(2) is one of the few deductions that is fully allowed in both the Old and New Tax Regimes.

## Section 24(b)

### What is Section 24(b)?
Section 24(b) allows taxpayers to claim a deduction for the interest paid on home loans taken for purchase, construction, repair, or reconstruction of residential property.

### What are the limits under Section 24(b)?
- **Self-Occupied Property**: A maximum deduction of ₹2,00,000 per financial year is allowed.
- **Let-out Property**: The entire interest paid can be claimed as a deduction (no upper limit). However, loss from house property that can be set off against other income heads is capped at ₹2,00,000 per year.

### Is Section 24(b) available in the New Tax Regime?
- **Self-Occupied Property**: No deduction is allowed for interest on home loans under the New Tax Regime.
- **Let-out Property**: Deduction is allowed, but losses cannot be set off against other heads of income.

## House Rent Allowance (HRA) Exemption

### What is House Rent Allowance (HRA) exemption?
Salaried individuals who live in rented accommodation can claim tax exemption on HRA received from their employer under Section 10(13A).

### How is the HRA exemption calculated?
The exemption is the minimum of the following three amounts:
1. Actual HRA received from the employer.
2. Actual Rent paid minus 10% of basic salary.
3. 50% of basic salary (for metro cities: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) or 40% of basic salary (for non-metro cities).

### Is HRA exemption available in the New Tax Regime?
No. HRA exemption under Section 10(13A) is not available in the New Tax Regime.

## Section 80GG

### What is Section 80GG?
Section 80GG provides a deduction for rent paid by taxpayers who do not receive House Rent Allowance (HRA) from their employer, or for non-salaried individuals.

### What are the limits under Section 80GG?
The deduction is the minimum of:
1. ₹5,000 per month (₹60,000 per year).
2. 25% of total income.
3. Actual Rent paid minus 10% of total income.
Note: The taxpayer, spouse, or minor child must not own residential accommodation at the place of duty.

### Is Section 80GG available in the New Tax Regime?
No. Section 80GG is only available under the Old Tax Regime.

---

## Section 80E

### What is Section 80E?
Section 80E offers a deduction for interest paid on education loans taken for higher studies of self, spouse, children, or a student for whom the taxpayer is the legal guardian.

### What are the limits under Section 80E?
There is no maximum monetary limit on the interest amount that can be claimed. You can claim deduction for the actual interest paid for a maximum of 8 years or until the interest is fully repaid, whichever is earlier.

### Is Section 80E available in the New Tax Regime?
No. Section 80E is not available under the New Tax Regime.

---

## Section 80TTA and 80TTB

### What are Sections 80TTA and 80TTB?
These sections provide deductions on interest income earned from savings accounts and fixed deposits.

### What are the limits?
- **Section 80TTA (Individuals < 60 years)**: Deduction of up to ₹10,000 on interest earned from savings accounts with banks, co-operative societies, or post offices.
- **Section 80TTB (Senior Citizens 60+ years)**: Deduction of up to ₹50,000 on interest earned from all deposits (savings, fixed deposits, recurring deposits) with banks, post offices, or co-operative societies.

### Are these available in the New Tax Regime?
No. Both Section 80TTA and 80TTB deductions are not available under the New Tax Regime.
